# Venue: Room 138, California Baptist University

> **The band, the rig's positions and the room's look are no longer this
> script's.** They were set with the programming actions
> (`examples/scripts/06-lab-band.luau`): running `tools/make_room138.py`
> again writes the older band and rig over them. The room's own model is
> `tools/build_lab138_room.py`'s, and edits to it are in
> `environment.edits.json`.

Generated by `tools/make_room138.py` — edit the script, not the JSON. Every
number in the files is derived from the room's dimensions, so a
correction ("the towers are at nine feet, not six") is one edit and a
re-run rather than thirty-six hand-written quaternions.

The room itself is measured: `environment.json` and the model it names
are built by `tools/build_lab138_room.py` from a LiDAR scan and
photographs. What was measured, how the model loads, and how the rig
below sits in the real room are in `docs/lighting/lab-138-room.md`.

    python3 tools/make_room138.py
    just shot /tmp/r138.png --venue data/venues/room138-cbu --camera Wide \
        --auto-exposure off --exposure 1.6

## The room

14.00 m along the curtain wall by 10.12 m deep (it was given as 48 by
35 ft, 0.6 m long both ways), the band along the curtain wall. In venue
coordinates (metres, x = stage right(−)/left(+), y = downstage(−)/
upstage(+), z = up, playing area centred on the origin):

| | |
|---|---|
| Width | 14.00 m, walls at x = ±7.0 |
| Depth | 10.12 m, band wall at y = +1.8288, house back at y = −8.2912 |
| Height | 3.50 m soffits at both ends; a tile pyramid to 5.45 m between them |
| Playing area | 12 ft deep, centred on y = 0 |

The band wall is a velvet curtain hung 12 cm off it, floor to 3.12 m.
The lounge is the stage-right end, the mixing console the stage-left end.

## The rig — 40 fixtures, 224 channels, universe 1

**4 towers, 24 lights.** Symmetric at x = ±6 ft and ±18 ft, just in front of
the band wall. Six lights stacked on each ~5 ft stand, touching, as
photographed. Each light is a warm-amber blinder strip across the middle of
a honeycomb lens with LED behind the rest of the face — `CBU Tower Blinder
Ring`, generated from `data/fixtures/cbu-tower-blinder-ring.json`, four
channels: WW (the blinder), then R/G/B (the surround).

**2 trees, 8 parcans.** T-bar stands about 8 ft tall at x = ±2.9 m, 14 ft
into the house, four pars side by side along each crossbar. (They were at
±16 ft, which in the real room is inside the mixing console on one side
and on the lounge's coffee table on the other.) `36 LED Par
Can`, 7 ch.

Each par is aimed at one player rather than washing the band flat. Left
to right on the audience-left tree: **keys, bass, drums, vocal**. The far
tree takes them in the opposite order, so every player gets light from
both sides — one key, one fill — instead of two pars from the same
direction. The fixture names carry the target (`Tree 1 Par 3 — Drums`),
so a repatch that moves one is obvious.

**4 movers.** In front of the band wall's curtain at 8 ft, above
the 5 ft towers so they clear the band's heads and can sweep the room.
Placed in the gaps between and outside the towers so the two packages
interleave rather than stack: −21, −18ᵀ, −12, −6ᵀ, +6ᵀ, +12, +18ᵀ, +21.
`150W Moving Head Beam`, 12 ch. Hung facing downstage with a little tilt
down; pan and tilt are live, so that is only where they sit at zero.

**4 floor bars.** Two at the foot of the band wall throwing up it
(`Wall Bars`). Two flanking the frontman, downstage of him and angled in
so his face is lit from both sides rather than flat from one (`Key
Bars`) — which is the reason there are two and not one. `Solena Max Bar
28 RGB`, 6 ch.

## The band

Left and right below are the **audience's**, which is the reading of
"left of the drums" this was built on. `AUDIENCE_LEFT` in the script is
the one constant that mirrors the whole band if that is wrong.

| | |
|---|---|
| Drums | 0.65 m audience-left of centre, 1.05 m off the band wall (measured), drummer seated |
| Bass | 6.5 ft audience-left of the kit |
| Guitars | 5.5 ft and 11 ft audience-right |
| Vocal | centre, 9 ft downstage of the wall, on a mic in front of the kit |
| Keys | 14 ft audience-left, further out and turned in 45° |

The frontman plays bass. **The bass is not modelled** — `assets/props/`
has a drum kit, a keyboard, a PA cabinet and a floor monitor, and no
stringed instrument — so he is a person on a mic stand, and the prop is
named `Person - Vocal Bass` so the intent survives until there is a
model to hang on it.

## Profile bindings

The NSYNC show targets **roles and never a group by name** (checked: zero
raw `Group` targets in `bye-bye-bye.json`), so what it looks like in this
room is entirely what these say:

| Role | Group | |
|---|---|---|
| `Key` | Tree Faces | two pars a side, on the singer and the lead guitarist, kept white |
| `Fill` | Tree Colour | the other two pars a side, free to take the song's colour |
| `Wash` | Towers | the main colourable surface, and the show's biggest target |
| `Back` | Wall Bars | up the wall behind the band |
| `Bars` | Bars | all four |
| `Floor` | Wall Bars | the uplight package |
| `Audience` | Tower Lamps | the twenty-four warm whites, which face the room |
| `Drums` | Drum Towers | the middle pair, which are what is actually over the kit |
| `Movers` | Movers | the four on the wall |
| `Beams` | Movers | being beam movers, they are the hard-edged package too |

Unbound, deliberately: **Haze, House Lights.** Room 138 has none of
them, and the show names them rarely — House Lights twice, the others not
at all. Those cues are skipped with a warning naming the role, which is
the graceful-degradation rule working rather than a fault.

Adding the movers closed the two gaps that actually mattered: `Movers` is
the show's third-largest target at 71 uses and `Beams` another 21, and
both were dark in this room before.

## Assumed, not measured

The room is measured. The rig is still from a description and two
photographs, and none of its forty fixtures is in the scan or the room's
photographs (`docs/lighting/lab-138-room.md` lists what that leaves
open), so the guesses are listed rather than buried:

- **Mover positions** — one on top of each tower, the owner's call: the
  best place this room has for them. Base at the top of the column.
- **Tower and tree positions** — "spread symmetrically" and "in the
  audience" were the whole of the brief. The x spacing, the distance into
  the house and the aim angles are chosen to look like a room that works,
  not measured.
- **The tower fixture** — identified from the owner's own desk file as
  the U'King ZQ01334 in its 15-channel mode: one level, one strobe and
  one colour for the whole tower, and a warm white for each of its six
  lamps. Each lamp is still its own record, drawn as a lamp
  (`CBU Tower Blinder Ring`), with `wired_as` naming the real fixture and
  `cell` its lamp. Lamp 0 (`Tower 1` to `Tower 4`, channels 41 to 44) is
  the tower itself: its level, strobe and colour, drawn by its lamps. So
  `Towers` is those four and `Tower Lamps` is the twenty-four warm
  whites, each with a level of its own; the output works out the one
  level byte the fixture really has. The drawn lamp's size, 40 degree spread and 18 W are still
  read off the photograph.
- **DMX addressing** — packed from 1 with no gaps, in the order towers,
  trees, movers, wall bars, key bars. Whatever the real patch is, it goes in
  `patch.json` and `fixtures.json` together.
- **Drum kit** — where the scan has it, borrowing Riverside's kit
  dimensions.

## Not modelled

No screens (`screens.json` is the empty list), no haze machine, no areas
grid, no palettes of its own — those come from the `Ignition` profile
until the room needs its own.
