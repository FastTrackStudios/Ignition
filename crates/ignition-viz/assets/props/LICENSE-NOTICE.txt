drum-kit.glb is "Full Drum Kit" by Batoski, obtained from Poly Pizza
(https://poly.pizza/m/1HvDgRF7fX) and released under Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0:

    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

CC-BY *does* impose a condition — attribution — unlike the CC0
characters in ../people. This file is that attribution, and it has to
travel with any build that ships the model. CC-BY 3.0 is compatible with
this repo's GPL-3.0-or-later: the licence stays on the asset, the
attribution stays with it, and neither is ours to change.

The file is the unmodified glTF binary as published.

Note on scale and orientation: the kit is authored in arbitrary units,
facing its own -Z, with its footprint off-origin. `props.rs` scales it
so its kick drum is a real 22" (0.559 m) rather than trusting the
bounding box, recentres it, and turns it to face the audience.

    floor-monitor.glb  "Floor Monitor" by Peter Simcoe
                       https://poly.pizza/m/2827m06VfIA

    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

All three are unmodified glTF binaries as published.

Like the kit, each is authored in arbitrary units and none is centred on
its own origin, so `props.rs` seats them by a real-world dimension
(`GlbProp::model_height`) and recentres them (`GlbProp::centre`) rather
than trusting a bounding box. Those constants were measured from the
files' own baked scene bounds and then corrected by eye — see the
`GLB_PROPS` table.
