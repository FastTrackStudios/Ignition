GDTF standard primitive meshes
==============================

The .3ds files in this directory are the official PrimitiveType meshes
shipped with the GDTF specification, copied verbatim from
https://github.com/mvrdevelopment/spec (folder `meshes/1.0` and
`meshes/1.1`, main branch, fetched 2026-08-26):

  primitivetype_base.3ds              PrimitiveType::Base
  primitivetype_yoke.3ds              PrimitiveType::Yoke
  primitivetype_head.3ds              PrimitiveType::Head
  primitivetype_scanner.3ds           PrimitiveType::Scanner
  primitivetype_conventional.3ds      PrimitiveType::Conventional
  primitivetype_base_1.1.3ds          PrimitiveType::Base1_1
  primitivetype_scanner_1.1.3ds       PrimitiveType::Scanner1_1
  primitivetype_conventional_1.1.3ds  PrimitiveType::Conventional1_1

License, per the repository README ("meshes" section):

  "They are free to use, modify, and distribute, including in commercial
   applications, without any licensing fees or royalties."

The spec ships no mesh for Cube, Cylinder, Sphere or Pigtail; those are
generated procedurally in src/gdtf_geometry.rs.
