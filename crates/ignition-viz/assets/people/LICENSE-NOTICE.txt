These character models are from Quaternius' "Ultimate Modular Men" pack
(https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0 Universal — public domain dedication, no attribution required:

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Quaternius' own statement for these packs: "Free to use in personal,
educational and commercial projects. (CC0 License)".

CC0 imposes no conditions, so this notice is courtesy rather than
obligation — the author is worth crediting and knowing where an asset
came from matters when someone later asks whether it can ship.

The files here are unmodified glTF binaries:

    man-casual.glb    Ultimate Modular Men, "Casual"
    man-casual-2.glb  Ultimate Modular Men, "Casual2"

Obtained from the mirror at
https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase
(public/glb/modular_men), because quaternius.com serves its downloads
through an itch.io page that cannot be fetched non-interactively. The
originals are the authoritative source; re-download from quaternius.com
if these ever need refreshing.

Note on orientation: glTF is Y-up and this project's world is Z-up (see
`view.rs`), so `props.rs` rotates these on the way in rather than
re-exporting the files. They are byte-identical to what Quaternius
published.

Note on animation: these packs ship a rig but no animation clips.
Quaternius' Universal Animation Library has them, but on a different
(Rigify `DEF-*`) skeleton whose bone names do not match these, so its
clips cannot be retargeted onto these characters without a rebind. The
two poses this venue needs are solved directly against the skeleton
instead — see `Pose` in `props.rs`.
